Verbal Town Notification

To receive Town Notification location alerts while using another app, such as Ride with GPS, first open Town Notification and click “Enable Location Alerts.” Then open the other app. Town Notification will continue monitoring your location in the background and will announce when you enter a different town or city.

When you enter a different town or city, an audio notification will be triggered.

Click "Enable Location Alerts" to start!

While Location Alerts are enabled, Android displays a small bold TN symbol in the status bar. The TN symbol confirms that Town Notification is continuing to monitor while another app is open or the screen is off. On Android 13 and later, notification permission is required so this background-running indicator can be shown.
