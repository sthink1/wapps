MY MONEY

These are places where you have money available for spending.

Examples include money in your wallet, checking and savings accounts used by debit cards, and the uninvested cash portion of an investment or brokerage account.

Example — Fidelity account #xxxxxxx
Money market funds = $562.12 <<< My Money
Certificates of Deposit = $5,000.00 << My Investment
Stocks and bonds = $21,928.20 << My Investment
Total value = $27,490.32

My Money consists of funds that are available for spending at essentially their stated dollar value. A dollar held as cash, in a checking account, savings account, or qualifying cash-equivalent account remains a dollar available to spend.

My Investments can generally be turned into My Money by selling, but their values can change before the sale.

MY INVESTMENTS

Value fluctuates. My Investments can be turned into My Money quickly by selling and are usually held at an investment company.

Example — Fidelity account #xxxxxxx
Money market funds = $562.12 <<< My Money
Certificates of Deposit = $5,000.00 << My Investment
Stocks and bonds = $21,928.20 << My Investment
Total value = $27,490.32

My Money = $562.12
My Investment = $26,928.20

Future planned proceeds from selling an investment should be recorded as a My Budget IN item.

Future planned cost of a purchase of an investment should be recorded as a My Budget OUT item.
