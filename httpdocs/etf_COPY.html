ETF

The goal of ETF Investment is to generate 20%+ annual return of investment by choosing securities that have a prior record of that rate of return or better. Then hold those securities for at least one year, sell them, and capture long term capital gains, which are taxed at a preferential rate.

Research and manage Exchange-Traded Fund investments. Select ETF categories, review and compare ETF symbols, and track ETF investment activity, including purchases, sales and holdings. Automatically calculate the number of shares to purchase based on an investment amount allocated to the ETFs you want to purchase.

This application does not give investment advice. Prior results do not guarantee future performance.
