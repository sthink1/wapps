Budget - Credit Card

When you are paying the card balance in full each month: The Budget assumes the balance is already represented by the underlying Out items. Do not enter a separate Out payment.

When you are NOT paying the card balance in full each month: check Include Monthly Payment in Budget. You may enter an Alternative Payment Amount greater than the Minimum Payment Due.

The information on this page should come from your credit card statement, except the Alternative Payment Amount, which you determine.
