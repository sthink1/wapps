Wonderful Apps Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

1. Information Wonderful Apps Receives

Wonderful Apps may receive account information such as user name, email address, telephone numbers, authentication information, subscription information, notification preferences, and information entered into individual applications.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide and secure accounts, operate Wonderful Apps applications, maintain subscriptions and entitlements, send requested or necessary notifications, respond to users, diagnose problems, and improve service operation.

3. Family Tree Information About Non-Users

A Wonderful Apps Family Tree user may enter information about another person who does not have a Wonderful Apps account. This may include an email address or other contact information. If an email address is associated with a Person profile, Wonderful Apps may use that address to send Family Tree notices about edits or deletion of that Person profile. The notice explains why it was sent and includes a way to stop future Family Tree notifications.

An email address appearing only in a Family Tree Person profile is not treated as permission to send Wonderful Apps marketing messages.

4. Notification Preferences

Registered users may choose whether to receive optional Wonderful Apps marketing messages and certain application notifications. Family Tree recipients, including non-users, may use the Stop Family Tree Notifications link in a notice. Stopping a notification category does not automatically delete the underlying email address from a Family Tree Person record.

5. Required Account Messages

Wonderful Apps may continue to send messages needed for account security, authentication, subscription status, or operation of a requested service even when optional marketing or application notifications are turned off.

6. Service Providers

Wonderful Apps uses service providers to host and operate portions of the service, send email, store data or files, and support application functions. Those providers may process information as necessary to perform those services.

7. Security

Wonderful Apps uses technical and organizational measures intended to protect information, including password hashing, authenticated access to protected functions, and server-side authorization controls. No Internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Retention

Information may be retained while needed to operate an account or application, maintain security and audit history, honor notification-suppression requests, resolve disputes, or meet applicable obligations. Notification suppression information may be retained so that a request to stop a category is not lost.

9. Choices and Requests

Available controls may allow registered users to change notification preferences and manage information within individual applications. A Family Tree recipient may stop future Family Tree notifications using the link in a notice.

10. Changes to This Policy

The Privacy Policy may be updated as Wonderful Apps changes. The effective date shown above identifies this version. Material changes may be presented to registered users or otherwise communicated as appropriate.

11. Contact

Privacy questions may be submitted through the Wonderful Apps contact function.
